Our Story

在縫隙中，尋找光芒

「隙光」這個名字，來自光線穿過縫隙時那種柔和而溫暖的質感。

我們相信，每個人都在生命中尋找屬於自己的光芒。有時候，光不在最明亮的地方，而是在那些被忽略的縫隙裡。

Finding Light in the Spaces Between

The name "Space Between" comes from the soft, warm quality of light filtering through cracks and openings.

We believe everyone is searching for their own light in life. Sometimes, light isn't in the brightest places, but in those overlooked spaces in between.

Space Between Healing is a healing and creative consulting studio. We accompany healers, coaches, mentors, creators, and service-based brands in finding clarity and direction through narrative guidance and strategic consulting.
